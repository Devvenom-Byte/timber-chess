Privacy policy

Timber Chess · last updated 23 November 2026

Timber Chess is a free chess app with no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no accounts. This page explains exactly what information it uses.

Information that stays on your device

Your settings, progress (lessons, puzzles, openings), saved games against the computer, the nickname you type, and a random device code are stored only in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent anywhere, except as described under "Playing online" below. Clearing the app's site data, or uninstalling it, deletes them.

Playing online with family and friends

Only if you use Family → Invite a friend or join an invite, the app stores that game in Google Firebase Realtime Database, hosted in the European Union (Belgium). For each online game it stores:

WhatWhy
The moves, any resignation or draw offer, and the last reaction emojiSo both players see the same game
The nickname each player typedSo you know who you're playing
A random device code for each player (not linked to your identity)To know which seat (White or Black) belongs to which device
When each player last had the game open (while the game is in progress)To show the green "online" dot

Anyone who has a game's invite link or code can see that game, including the nicknames. Only share invite links with people you want to play. Please use a nickname or first name, not a full name.

Old games are removed from the database from time to time. To have a game deleted sooner, contact us (see below) with its invite code.

Services the app relies on

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the app. Like any website host, it may briefly log technical information such as your IP address for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Firebase stores online games as described above. Google processes your IP address to deliver the data. See Firebase's privacy information.

The app's fonts and pictures are included with the app, so no other third parties are contacted.

Children

Timber Chess has no chat, no public profiles, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The only personal information involved is the nickname typed for online games, which is shown to the other player in that game. Parents can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data.

Your rights

You can ask what information about you is held in an online game, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Most of it is on your own device and you can delete it yourself by clearing the app's data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/Devvenom-Byte/timber-chess.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
